Oracle Commerce Cloud (OCC) is a robust, scalable e-commerce platform for managing online storefronts. It enables businesses to design and manage storefronts, handle product catalogs, orders, customers, and integrate advanced marketing tools and analytics.

Key Features

  • Storefront Design: Customizable templates for creating online storefronts, managing product catalogs, and configuring payment/shipping options.
  • Product Catalog Management: Comprehensive tools for handling product details, variations, bundles, and reviews.
  • Order Management: Full order lifecycle management, including returns and cancellations.
  • Customer Management: Tools for customer segmentation, personalized marketing, and profile management.
  • Marketing & Promotions: Email campaigns, social media integrations, and targeted promotions.
  • Analytics: Extensive reporting tools to track sales, customer behavior, and marketing performance.

Widgets

Widgets provide the building blocks for OCC functionalities, including templates, JavaScript, CSS, locales, and configurable settings for flexibility in design and functionality.

Setup & Integration

OCC integrates with Dell Boomi and Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) for seamless cloud and on-premises application integration. Steps include creating connections, defining data mapping, and automating workflows to enhance efficiency.
